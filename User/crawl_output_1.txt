#### The 'Praan' of it all
Praan (the wild Kashmiri shallot) is pretty much the life of Wazwan. This one ingredient has the capacity to make our break how the food tastes because it forms the base of all of our gravies (yes, no onion or tomatoes). Our food is also exclusively cooked in ghee.
